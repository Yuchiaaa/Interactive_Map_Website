Environmental Risk Score

Each livestock farm receives a composite 0–100 risk score combining NH₃ emission intensity, proximity to Natura 2000 protected areas, nearby pesticide exceedance, and the density of schools and health facilities within 5 km.

Composite index scoring with four components, each normalised to [0, 1] before weighting:

  • NH₃ emissions (30%) — percentile rank of raw kg/yr from KRD
  • Natura 2000 proximity (30%) — 1 / (1 + dist_km / 5); closest = highest risk; computed via geopandas sjoin_nearest in EPSG:28992
  • Pesticide exceedance (25%) — normalised average at the nearest monitoring station (KNN lookup in PostGIS)
  • Sensitive receptors (15%) — percentile rank of schools + health facilities within 5 km (PostGIS ST_DWithin)

Final score = Σ(weight × component) × 100

Data sources: KRD Veehouderijen, Bestrijdingsmiddelenatlas, Natura 2000 (PDOK), DUO scholen, HOTOSM health facilities
Sources: KRD · Bestrijdingsmiddelenatlas · Natura 2000 (PDOK) · DUO · HOTOSM

Pesticide Exceedance Trends

For each monitoring station with ≥ 3 years of data, a Mann-Kendall monotonic trend test determines whether peak exceedance is significantly worsening, improving, or stable over time.

Mann-Kendall monotonic trend test (non-parametric, no distributional assumptions):

  • Computed via scipy.stats.kendalltau(years, peak_exceedance)
  • Significance threshold: p < 0.05
  • τ > 0, p < 0.05 → increasing (worsening)
  • τ < 0, p < 0.05 → decreasing (improving)
  • p ≥ 0.05 → stable

Theil-Sen slope gives the estimated change in exceedance ratio per year — a robust slope estimate resistant to outliers.

Minimum 3 years of data required per station.
Source: Bestrijdingsmiddelenatlas (RIVM / water boards)

Isolation Forest (scikit-learn, 200 trees, contamination = 5%):

  • Features: NH₃ (kg/yr), geur (ouE/s), fijnstof (g/yr) — all log1p-transformed to reduce right-skew
  • Isolation Forest isolates anomalies by randomly partitioning the feature space; anomalies require fewer splits to isolate
  • decision_function score: higher = more normal, lower = more anomalous
  • Farms predicted as −1 (≈ bottom 5%) are flagged is_anomaly = True

Anomalous farms are not necessarily violating regulations — they are statistically unusual and may warrant further investigation or data verification.
Source: KRD Veehouderijen (Omgevingsdiensten)
