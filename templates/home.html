Workflow

How It Works

Explore environmental data and generate exportable evidence in just four simple steps.

Step 01
Explore Datasets
Turn on any of the 13 data layers from the sidebar. Search datasets by name and quickly view where the data comes from, what it covers, and how often it is updated.
Step 02
Inspect Features
Click on any map feature to open detailed information, including connected data from other active layers and a direct link to the original source.
Step 03
Run Spatial Analysis
Create buffer zones around locations and identify overlaps between datasets.
Step 04
Export Findings
Download your results as PDF reports or Excel files to support legal cases, research projects, investigations, or public reporting.
