How It Works

Explore environmental data and generate exportable evidence in just four simple steps.

Step 01
Explore Datasets
Turn on any of the 13 data layers from the sidebar. Search datasets by name and quickly view where the data comes from, what it covers, and how often it is updated.
Step 02
Inspect Features
Click on any map feature to open detailed information, including connected data from other active layers and a direct link to the original source.
Step 03
Run Spatial Analysis
Create buffer zones around locations and identify overlaps between datasets.
Step 04
Export Findings
Download your results as PDF reports or Excel files to support legal cases, research projects, investigations, or public reporting.

Integrated Spatial & Environmental Datasets

13 publicly available environmental monitoring and governmental data sources integrated into one interactive map.

Agriculture
Basisregistratie Gewaspercelen (BRP)
Registers all agricultural parcels and their declared crops across the Netherlands each year. Used for EU subsidy administration and agricultural policy monitoring.

Infrastructure
Basisregistraties Adressen en Gebouwen (BAG)
The national register of all addresses and buildings in the Netherlands, including construction year, usage type, and official status.
SourcePDOK

Biodiversity
Natura 2000 Areas
EU-designated protected nature areas under the Birds and Habitats Directives. Activities within or near these boundaries are subject to strict environmental permit requirements.
SourcePDOK
↗ View Source
Biodiversity
Natuurnetwerk Nederland (NNN)
A national network of nature areas aimed at protecting and connecting biodiversity, defined per province under the Dutch Nature Protection Act.
SourceNationaal Georegister
↗ View Source
Cadastre
Cadastral Data
Official cadastral parcel boundaries and sizes across the Netherlands, essential for identifying land ownership in legal and environmental disputes.
SourceNationaal Georegister
↗ View Source
Emissions
KRD Veehouderijen
Registered livestock farms in the Netherlands with their NH₃ (ammonia), odour, and particulate matter emission values. Used in legal assessments of cumulative farm impact on nature areas.

Pesticides
Bestrijdingsmiddelen Atlas

Administration
Bestuurlijke Gebieden
Administrative boundaries of municipal, provincial, and national borders, compiled based on the cadastral register (BRK). This dataset contains the history.

Water
Water Authorities Hydrography
INSPIRE-harmonised watercourse data from Dutch water authorities (waterschappen), covering rivers, canals, and drainage channels with stream order and condition attributes.

WFD Surface Water
Water Framework Directive (WFD) surface water body boundaries covering rivers, lakes, and coastal waters assessed for ecological and chemical status under EU law.
